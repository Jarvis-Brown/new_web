Overview

Fit For You is a mobile fitness app concept designed to help users create personalized workout routines from the comfort of their homes. The app supports different fitness levels, from beginners to experienced users, by offering tailored exercise recommendations, customizable workout schedules, and progress tracking.

The goal was to create an accessible and flexible fitness experience for people who may struggle to visit a gym due to busy schedules, cost, or personal comfort.

Role

UI/UX Designer

Tools

Adobe XD / Photoshop / After Effects

Challenge

Many people struggle to maintain consistent workout routines due to busy schedules, gym membership costs, or discomfort exercising in public spaces. While at-home fitness apps provide an alternative, users may still struggle to find workouts that match their experience level, personal goals, and availability.

The challenge was to design a flexible fitness experience that helps users discover suitable exercises, build personalized schedules, and track their progress without feeling overwhelmed.

Competition

Fitbod

  • Doesn't ask questions to help guide the user to the best possible workout. Fit For You asks a series of questions to provide suggested workout options for its users.

BetterMen

  • Provides user with workout programs instead of letting the user create their own workouts. Users may be looking for specific workouts that aren't available in the program. Fit For You suggests workouts and gives the user the option to choose which exercise they would like to perform.

Home Workout Fitness

  • Doesn't allow users to time their workout sessions. Users would like to view how long their sessions are to see progress over time. Fit For You lets users start and stop your sessions and view past workout sessions to see their progress.

Personas

Fit For You targets users between the ages of 21 to 44. This group is most likely to work out and at that age may not have a lot of free time due to work or school.

User Needs

To succeed with an at-home fitness routine, users identified the following key needs:

  • Track and view created workout schedules on a calendar
  • Time their workout sessions
  • Have the ability to modify workout exercises
  • Track workout progress
  • View tips on how to properly execute selected exercise

Solution

I designed Fit For You to make at-home fitness more approachable through personalized workout recommendations and flexible scheduling. During onboarding, users answer questions about their fitness goals and experience level, helping them discover exercises suited to their needs.

The app allows users to customize their workout routines, schedule sessions around their availability, and track their progress over time. Exercise tutorials and wellness videos provide additional guidance, creating a fitness experience that supports users beyond their workouts.

User Flow

I created a user flow to map out how someone would move through Fit For You, from setting up their profile to completing a workout. The goal was to make the experience straightforward, allowing users to discover exercises, customize their routines, and schedule workouts without unnecessary steps.

The flow helped establish the app's navigation and identify the key screens needed before moving into wireframing.

  • Answer questions to determine workout
  • Create profile
  • Select exercises
  • Browse tutorials
  • Select dates to exercise
  • Exercise

Low Fidelity Wireframes

I created low-fidelity wireframes for iOS and Android to explore the app's layout, navigation, and core functionality before moving into visual design. These early designs focused on creating a straightforward experience for users to set their fitness preferences, schedule workouts, and monitor their progress.
Designing for both platforms also allowed me to consider differences in their interface conventions while maintaining a consistent experience.

Native App Designs

I developed a visual style for Fit For You that focused on keeping the interface clean, approachable, and easy to navigate. I selected typography, colors, and icons to establish consistency across the iOS and Android designs while making important features and actions easy to recognize.
The goal was to create a cohesive experience that would feel welcoming to users with different fitness levels.

User Feedback

After creating the iOS and Android prototypes, I gathered feedback from several users to evaluate the clarity, usability, and overall design of the app.

Their feedback highlighted opportunities to improve content consistency, visual inclusivity, and the visibility of important workout information. These observations helped identify areas that could be refined to create a more intuitive experience.

    User 1:

  • The user noticed that the home screen's title didn't align with the healthy eating video being displayed. They suggested updating the title to create a clearer connection between the content and its purpose.

    User 2:

Final Study

Takeaways

Designing Fit For You helped me better understand how important personalization and clear navigation are when creating a fitness experience for users with different goals and experience levels.

Through prototype feedback, I identified opportunities to improve content consistency, make important workout information more visible, and create a more inclusive interface. These observations reinforced the importance of testing designs with users and considering their perspectives throughout the design process.

If I continued developing the concept, I would explore additional nutrition tracking features to help users monitor their eating habits alongside their workout routines.
